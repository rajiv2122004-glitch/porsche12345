Engineering with a sense of occasion

Porsche is a German sports car maker founded in Stuttgart, with the Porsche family’s first production car, the 356, arriving in 1948.

The 911 has kept its rear-engine layout and round headlights for six decades, and remains the heart of the range.

Motorsport shapes the road cars: Porsche is one of the most successful brands in endurance racing.

The Taycan, launched in 2019, brought the brand’s first fully electric sports car to India.
